CV Examples for Every Industry & Job Role in 2026

By ShopMart Team • Updated February 20, 2026 • 8 min read

If you are struggling to write your Curriculum Vitae (CV), the best place to start is by looking at proven examples. Seeing how successful candidates structure their experience, skills, and education can give you the blueprint you need to land the interview.

In this guide, we've compiled professional CV examples across different industries and experience levels, along with tips on what makes each one effective.

What is a CV (and How is it Different from a Resume)?

In the US and Canada, a "CV" (Curriculum Vitae) is usually a long, academic document used for research or teaching roles. A "resume" is a short 1-2 page marketing document.

However, in the UK, Europe, India, and Australia, the terms "CV" and "Resume" are often used interchangeably to mean a 1-2 page document used for job applications. The examples below are for standard 1-2 page job application CVs.

What Makes a Great CV? 5 Key Elements

  1. Clear Structure: Standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills).
  2. Tailored Content: Keywords matched to the job description.
  3. Measurable Achievements: Numbers, percentages, and metrics.
  4. Professional Formatting: Single-column, ATS-friendly, clean fonts.
  5. No Errors: Flawless spelling, grammar, and punctuation.

Conclusion

Using a proven CV example as your starting point can save you hours of formatting and writing. Pick the example that best matches your industry and experience level, adapt it with your own achievements, and tailor it for every job you apply to.
